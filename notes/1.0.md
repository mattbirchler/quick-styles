Quick Styles adds your own CSS to any website in Safari.

- Click the Quick Styles button in the toolbar, type CSS, and the page updates as you type.
- Styles are saved per site, and an **All sites** tab holds CSS that applies everywhere.
- Switch a site's styles off and on to compare with the original.
- **Manage all styles** lists every site you've styled, with export and import for backups.

It's free and open source, with no accounts and no tracking.
